How to play

Press anywhere and drag: up = higher arc, down = lower, right = more power, left = less. Each drag starts from your last aim. Release to shoot (a tap does nothing). Shots always fly straight at your rival; the wind flag shows a tail wind (carries further) or head wind (falls short). Low shots hit the merlon in front of your rival (2 hits, or 1 heavy arrow, knock it off) – lob over it or break it. Split arrows split into a high, middle and low arrow at the top of the arc. Pick an arrow: Normal 25 / head 50, Heavy 35 / 70 (drops faster, ignores most wind), Split splits into 3 at the top of its arc, 15 / 30 each.
